39. (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, tax shall be
assessed in the currency of transaction.
(2) Tax, including royalty, assessed in a currency other than the Nigerian
Naira shall be paid in that currency, or the Nigerian Naira at the prevailing
exchange rate in the official foreign exchange market.
(3) In the case of any return under this Act relating to petroleum operations,
all computations relating to tax and payments shall be in US Dollars.
